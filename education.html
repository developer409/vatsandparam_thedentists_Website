League of Dentists

Bridging Dental Education and Real-World Clinical Success

The League of Dentists was founded with a simple belief: exceptional dentists are shaped through mentorship, not just education.

Founded by Dr. Srivats Bharadwaj and co-founded by Dr. Paramjot Kaur, the initiative bridges the gap between dental education and modern clinical practice. Through structured mentorship, real-world learning, and personalised guidance, it equips students and early-career dentists with the confidence, clinical judgment, and communication skills required to build meaningful and successful careers.

What We Offer

What the League Provides

Clinical Mentorship

One-on-one and group mentoring to build case confidence, clinical decision-making, and procedural fluency.

Practice & Business Training

Frameworks for ethical practice growth, team leadership, workflow systems, and financial sustainability.

Communication & Case Acceptance

Master the language of dentistry — patient psychology, consultation frameworks, and case presentation skills.

Career Strategy & Growth Planning

Structured roadmaps for specialists, generalists, and academic-track dentists to build purposeful careers.

Workshops for Dental Colleges

Tailored workshops for BDS & MDS students covering clinical readiness, communication, and professional identity.

Peer Learning Community

A curated network of mentors and peers for accountability, shared learning, and long-term professional support.
