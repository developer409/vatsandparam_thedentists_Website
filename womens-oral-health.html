Functional Dentistry

Women's Oral Health Centre

Oral health care that understands the female body across every life stage.

A woman's oral health is shaped by her hormonal life. From puberty through pregnancy, perimenopause, and beyond, fluctuating oestrogen and progesterone levels create distinct oral health patterns that standard dental care often ignores. Increased gum sensitivity, pregnancy gingivitis, dry mouth during menopause, and bone density changes after oestrogen decline are all real, clinically significant, and manageable when properly understood.

“The female body is not a variation of the male body. It is its own biological system with its own oral health needs, and we are built to address them.”

The Women's Programme

What Does It Involve?

Women's Oral Health at Vats & Param is not a marketing category but a clinical specialisation. We assess hormonal oral health patterns at every life stage and integrate our dental management with your wider medical picture. Coordination with gynaecologists, obstetricians, endocrinologists, and osteoporosis specialists ensures that dental decisions support your overall health trajectory.

Our care in this centre is provided with sensitivity, privacy, and a recognition that the dental chair is often the first place some of these conversations happen. We are a safe, informed, and non-judgmental space for women to discuss oral health concerns they may not have felt able to raise elsewhere.

7 Women's Oral Health Services

01 / 07

Adolescent Oral Health

Guidance for teenage girls on the oral health changes that accompany puberty, including hormonal gum sensitivity and diet-related risks.

02 / 07

Pregnancy Dental Care

Safe, evidence-based dental assessment and treatment planning throughout pregnancy, including management of pregnancy gingivitis and morning sickness erosion.

03 / 07

Maternal Oral Health

Education and monitoring for expectant and new mothers on the links between their oral health, systemic health, and infant oral health outcomes.

04 / 07

Menopause & Oral Health

Assessment and management of menopausal oral changes including burning mouth, dry mouth, gum recession, and bone loss related to oestrogen decline.

05 / 07

Hormonal Oral Health Assessments

Structured evaluations that map your current life stage to your oral health presentation, identifying hormonal contributions to dental symptoms.

06 / 07

Dry Mouth Management

Identification and management of xerostomia caused by medication, hormonal changes, or systemic conditions, including salivary substitute and stimulant options.

07 / 07

Bone Health Considerations

Assessment of osteoporosis risk and its oral implications including jawbone density, implant candidacy, and the effects of bisphosphonate medication.
