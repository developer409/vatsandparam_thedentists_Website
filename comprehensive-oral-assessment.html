Comprehensive Oral Assessment

A thorough, evidence-based examination that reveals the complete picture of your oral and systemic health.

Book a Consultation

Most dental issues go undetected until they cause pain. Our comprehensive oral assessment is designed to identify risk factors, early disease, and systemic links before problems escalate. It is the foundation of all care at Vats & Param.

Teeth & Decay Analysis

Teeth & Decay Analysis

Digital X-rays and visual examination to detect cavities, erosion, and structural weakness before they require major intervention.

Gum & Bone Health

Gum & Bone Health

Periodontal charting to measure gum pocket depth, bone levels, and inflammation markers linked to systemic conditions.

Bite & Jaw (TMJ)

Bite & Jaw (TMJ)

Assessment of occlusion, jaw joint function, and signs of bruxism or clenching that can cause pain, headaches, and tooth wear.

Oral Cancer Screening

Oral Cancer Screening

Systematic soft tissue examination of lips, cheeks, tongue, floor of mouth, palate, and throat for any abnormal lesions.
