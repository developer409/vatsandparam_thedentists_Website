Overview

What is Cosmetic Dentistry?

Cosmetic dentistry is focused on enhancing the aesthetic appearance of teeth, gums, and your overall smile. By using the latest techniques and materials, we help you achieve a more confident look, which can positively impact your self-esteem and quality of life.

Whether it's subtle changes or a complete transformation, we provide personalized solutions designed to restore function and deliver an attractive, radiant smile.

Support

Maintaining Your New Smile

Care for your new restorations as you would natural teeth. Long-lasting beauty requires a consistent care routine.

  • Good Oral Hygiene: Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss to remove bacteria hiding between teeth.
  • Avoid Hard Foods: For the first few days post-treatment, stick to soft foods to avoid damaging new work.
  • Quit Smoking: Smoking stains teeth and affects gum health, compromising the longevity of your cosmetic work.
  • Limit Staining Items: Reduce intake of coffee, tea, and red wine to help maintain the brightness of your teeth.
  • Regular Check-ups: Schedule periodic visits to ensure your dental work is maintained and identified early.
  • Wear a Mouthguard: Protect your work from sports injuries or nighttime grinding with a custom mouthguard.
  • Follow Specific Instructions: Adhere to the unique aftercare plan provided by your doctor for your specific procedure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Beyond a beautiful smile, cosmetic dentistry can improve your oral function, prevent future dental issues, and significantly boost your self-confidence and professional appearance.

We offer a wide range of services including teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, composite bonding, gum contouring, and orthodontic aligners, depending on your individual goals.

Most cosmetic procedures are minimally invasive. We use gentle techniques and local anesthesia when necessary to ensure you are comfortable throughout the process.

Timelines vary: teeth whitening can take an hour, while veneers typically require two visits. We will provide a detailed timeline during your initial consultation.

Consistency is key. Maintain excellent home hygiene, attend regular professional cleanings, and avoid habits that can stain or chip your new restorations.
