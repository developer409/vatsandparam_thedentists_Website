Oral Medicine

Oral Medicine Centre

Specialist care for complex oral conditions, mucosal diseases, and conditions where dentistry meets systemic medicine.

Book a Consultation

Our Oral Medicine Centre addresses conditions that go beyond conventional dentistry, including complex mucosal disorders, chronic oral pain, salivary gland conditions, and oral manifestations of systemic diseases. We bridge the gap between dental and medical care.

Mucosal Disease Management

Mucosal Disease Management

Diagnosis and management of oral mucosal conditions including lichen planus, pemphigus, and recurrent aphthous ulcers with specialist-level care.

Chronic Oral Pain

Chronic Oral Pain

Thorough evaluation and management of unexplained or persistent oral facial pain, burning mouth syndrome, and neuropathic conditions.

Salivary Gland Disorders

Salivary Gland Disorders

Assessment and treatment of dry mouth (xerostomia), salivary gland infections, and swellings with appropriate clinical protocols.

Systemic Disease Links

Systemic Disease Links

Identifying and managing oral manifestations of systemic conditions such as diabetes, anaemia, autoimmune disorders, and gastrointestinal disease.
