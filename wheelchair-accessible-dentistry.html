Accessibility & Inclusion

Dental Care for Wheelchair-Bound Patients

We believe everyone deserves access to world-class dental care. Our clinics are equipped with specialized wheelchair recliner dental chairs to ensure a seamless, comfortable experience.

Inclusive Technology

The Compact Wheelchair Recliner Dental Chair

For wheelchair-bound patients, visiting the dentist can often be a challenge. Many dental offices are not designed to accommodate patients in wheelchairs, and traditional dental chairs can be uncomfortable or impossible for them to use. We have integrated advanced wheelchair-based treatment solutions at all our clinics.

What is it?

A compact wheelchair recliner dental chair is designed to provide comfort and accessibility to wheelchair-bound patients during dental procedures. Smaller in size than traditional units, it has the ability to recline, allowing patients to remain seated in their own wheelchair while receiving treatment. The compact design also makes it easier for our specialists to maneuver and provide high-precision care without requiring stressful transfers.

Why it Matters

Benefits of Accessible Care

Improved Comfort
