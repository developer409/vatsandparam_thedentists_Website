The Service

What is Teeth Whitening?

Teeth whitening is a non-invasive cosmetic dental treatment that aims to remove stains and discoloration, resulting in a significantly brighter and more attractive smile.

While there are many over-the-counter options, professional whitening provides superior results using safe, clinical-grade materials that protect your enamel while delivering maximum radiance.

Why Do Teeth Discolor?

Understanding the source of your stains helps us choose the most effective whitening protocol for you.

Life's Habits

Life's Habits

Frequent consumption of coffee, tea, red wine, and tobacco are the most common causes of surface stains.

Aging

Aging

Over time, the outer enamel wears down, revealing the naturally more yellowish dentin layer beneath.

Poor Oral Hygiene

Poor Oral Hygiene

Inadequate brushing and flossing can lead to plaque buildup, which traps stains and dulls appearance.

Trauma

Trauma

Injury to a tooth can cause the pulp to react, leading to internal darkening that requires specific attention.

Genetics

Genetics

Some individuals naturally have thicker or whiter enamel than others, influencing the baseline shade.

Medications

Medications

Certain antibiotics or medications during tooth development can cause intrinsic discoloration.

Support

Maintaining Your Brilliance

Extend the life of your whitening treatment by following these simple post-care steps.

  • The 'White Diet': Avoid staining foods/drinks (coffee, tea, wine, berries) for at least 24 hours after treatment.
  • Use a Straw: When drinking staining beverages, use a straw to minimize contact with your front teeth.
  • Consistent Hygiene: Brush and floss regularly to prevent new surface stains from forming.
  • Sensitivity Care: Use a sensitive-formula toothpaste if you experience temporary tooth sensitivity after treatment.
  • Tobacco-Free: Refrain from smoking or tobacco products, as these quickly re-stain your teeth.
  • Periodic Touch-ups: Consider maintenance treatments every few months or as recommended to keep results fresh.
  • Regular Cleanings: Schedule professional cleanings twice a year to maintain optimal oral health and shade.
