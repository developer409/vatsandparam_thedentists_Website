Our Technology

Precision Through Advanced Technology

We invest in diagnostic and treatment technology that serves a clear clinical purpose enhancing accuracy, safety, and patient outcomes.

3D Cone Beam CT (CBCT) Imaging

CBCT technology produces detailed three-dimensional images of teeth, bone, nerve pathways, and soft tissues. This allows us to visualize anatomy with extraordinary precision, leading to more accurate diagnoses and safer treatment planning.

Precision Benefit: Reveals hidden pathology and anatomical details invisible on traditional 2D X-rays, reducing diagnostic uncertainty.

Intraoral Cameras & Digital Microscopy

High-magnification intraoral cameras capture detailed images of tooth surfaces, margins, and soft tissues. These images allow both the clinician and the patient to see exactly what is happening, supporting transparent diagnosis and informed decision-making.

Precision Benefit: Enables early detection of micro-fractures, decay, and marginal discrepancies before they become major problems.

Laser-Assisted Dentistry

Dental lasers enable precise, minimally invasive treatment of both hard and soft tissues. From cavity preparation to gum therapy, laser technology reduces discomfort, minimizes bleeding, and accelerates healing compared to traditional methods.

Precision Benefit: Sub-millimeter accuracy in tissue management, preserving maximum healthy structure.

CAD/CAM Digital Restorations

Computer-aided design and manufacturing allows us to create precise dental restorations crowns, inlays, and onlays with digital accuracy. This technology ensures impeccable fit, natural aesthetics, and significantly reduced treatment time.

Precision Benefit: Micron-level accuracy in restoration fit, reducing the risk of margin gaps and secondary decay.

League of Dentists

Bridging Dental Education and Real-World Clinical Success

The League of Dentists is a structured professional mentorship initiative founded by Dr. Srivats Bharadwaj and co-founded by Dr. Paramjot Kaur. It is dedicated to transforming dental students and early-career practitioners into confident, practice-ready professionals bridging the gap between academic training and real-world clinical excellence. Through a personalised and structured approach, the League empowers dentists to lead with clarity, communicate with authority, and build careers of lasting impact.
