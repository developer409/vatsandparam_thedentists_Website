Orthodontics

Orthodontics & Bite Development

Correcting tooth alignment and bite function for improved aesthetics, oral health, and long-term structural wellbeing.

Book a Consultation

Orthodontic treatment is not just about straight teeth. Correct alignment impacts bite function, jaw health, breathing, speech, and the long-term integrity of teeth and supporting bone. Our approach considers the full functional picture alongside aesthetics.

Traditional Braces

Traditional Braces

Metal and ceramic bracket systems that provide precise, reliable correction of complex misalignments, suitable for children, teens, and adults.

Clear Aligner Therapy

Clear Aligner Therapy

Removable, near-invisible aligners that gradually move teeth into position, popular for adults seeking discreet orthodontic correction.

Bite Correction

Bite Correction

Treatment of overbites, underbites, crossbites, and open bites that affect jaw mechanics, chewing efficiency, and long-term joint health.

Functional & Growth Appliances

Functional & Growth Appliances

For growing children, functional appliances can guide jaw development and reduce the need for extraction or complex adult treatment later.
