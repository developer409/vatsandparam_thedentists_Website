Clinical Treatments

Restorative & Rehabilitative Dentistry

Rebuilding teeth that have been damaged, worn, or lost over time.

Restorative dentistry is the craft of repairing and rebuilding teeth that have been affected by decay, fracture, wear, or previous dental work. At Vats & Param, restoration goes beyond sealing a cavity; it involves understanding why the tooth was lost or damaged, addressing the cause, and rebuilding in a way that lasts. From simple fillings to complete oral rehabilitation, we match the complexity of the solution to the complexity of the problem.

“A tooth restored without understanding why it broke will break again. Every restoration begins with a diagnosis, not a drill.”

The Restorative Programme

What Does It Involve?

Restorative treatment planning at Vats & Param begins with a full assessment of your oral environment, including your bite, gum health, and the factors that led to the initial breakdown. We use digital radiography, photographs, and study models to plan restorations that restore both form and function within the context of your whole dental system.

Materials are selected for biocompatibility, longevity, and aesthetics. We use tooth-coloured composites and ceramics that bond well to tooth structure and match your natural appearance. Full-mouth rehabilitation cases are planned in phases, with clear communication and written treatment plans provided before any work begins.

8 Restorative Services

01 / 08

Tooth-Coloured Fillings

Direct composite resin restorations that restore decayed or fractured teeth to their original form and function with excellent aesthetics.

02 / 08

Inlays & Onlays

Precision-crafted ceramic or composite restorations for moderate-to-large cavities that provide greater strength and longevity than direct fillings.

03 / 08

Crowns

Full-coverage ceramic or zirconia restorations that protect and restore structurally compromised teeth after fracture, decay, or root canal treatment.

04 / 08

Bridges

Fixed ceramic restorations that replace missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth, restoring appearance, function, and bite stability.

05 / 08

Tooth Wear Rehabilitation

Comprehensive diagnosis and restoration of teeth damaged by acid erosion, bruxism, or abrasion, rebuilding lost vertical dimension and protecting remaining structure.

06 / 08

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Complete reconstruction of the bite using a combination of crowns, veneers, implants, and other restorations to restore aesthetics, function, and confidence.

07 / 08

Fracture Management

Emergency and elective management of fractured and cracked teeth, including assessment of restorability and appropriate treatment pathway selection.

08 / 08

Cosmetic Bonding

Conservative application of composite resin to close gaps, repair chips, change shape, or improve the appearance of teeth with minimal tooth reduction.

Our Approach

Three Principles Behind Every Appointment

01

Cause Before Cure

We identify and address the reason a tooth failed before restoring it. Treatment without diagnosis produces short-lived results.

02

Material Intelligence

We select restorative materials based on the clinical demands of each situation, not convenience or cost. Longevity is always the primary consideration.

03

Minimally Invasive

We remove only what cannot be saved. Conservative dentistry that preserves natural tooth structure is always preferable to more extensive treatment.
