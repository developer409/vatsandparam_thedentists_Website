Special Needs Dentistry Centre

Inclusive, adapted, compassionate dental care for every ability and need.

Dental care for people with special needs requires more than patience — it requires specific clinical skills, adapted environments, and a genuine commitment to inclusion. At Vats & Param, we have built our practice to be accessible, sensory-considerate, and clinically equipped to provide high-quality dental care to people with physical, intellectual, cognitive, and sensory disabilities, and those with complex behavioural needs.

“Every person deserves excellent dental care. Adapting how we deliver it is not optional for us — it is fundamental to who we are.”

The Special Needs Programme

What Does It Involve?

Our special needs dentistry is grounded in evidence-based behaviour guidance, communication adaptations, and a calm clinical environment. We take the time required for each individual to feel safe, understood, and in control of their experience. For people requiring pre-medication, sedation, or hospital-based dental care, we have established referral pathways to ensure that complex needs are met.

We work closely with caregivers, support workers, therapists, and family members, recognising that the best outcomes arise from collaborative care. Education and home care training for caregivers is a core part of what we provide, because consistent daily oral hygiene is the foundation of long-term oral health for everyone.

7 Special Needs Services

01 / 07

Autism-Friendly Dentistry

Adapted dental care for individuals on the autism spectrum, including sensory accommodations, visual scheduling, gradual desensitisation, and a predictable clinic environment.

02 / 07

Cerebral Palsy Care

Dental management adapted for the postural, motor, and communicative needs of individuals with cerebral palsy, including positioning aids and modified instruments.

03 / 07

Intellectual Disability Care

Individually adapted dental assessments and treatment for people with intellectual disabilities, with particular attention to communication and patient assent.

04 / 07

Behaviour Guidance

Evidence-based non-pharmacological techniques to support dental care for individuals with challenging behaviours, including tell-show-do, distraction, and positive reinforcement.

05 / 07

Sensory-Friendly Dentistry

Reduced stimulation appointments with adjustments for light, sound, smell, and touch for individuals with sensory processing differences.

06 / 07

Caregiver Education

Practical training and guidance for carers, support workers, and family members on daily oral hygiene techniques adapted for the specific needs of their person.

07 / 07

Preventive Programs

Structured prevention-focused dental care plans that minimise the need for treatment by prioritising risk reduction and regular maintenance.

Our Approach

Three Principles Behind Every Appointment

01

Inclusion Without Exception

We do not place a lower ceiling on the quality of care available to people with disabilities. Every person receives our best.

02

Adapted, Not Compromised

Adapting how we work does not compromise what we achieve. Our clinical standards remain consistent; our delivery is tailored to the individual.

03

Caregiver as Partner

Good oral health for people with disabilities requires daily care at home. We educate and support caregivers as essential members of the clinical team.
