Tobacco Cessation Programme

Comprehensive support to help you quit tobacco and reverse its damaging effects on your oral and overall health.

Book a Consultation

Tobacco use is one of the most significant risk factors for oral cancer, gum disease, tooth loss, and systemic illness. Our tobacco cessation programme combines motivational counselling, nicotine replacement guidance, and oral health monitoring to support you through every step of quitting.
