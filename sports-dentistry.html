Functional Dentistry

Sports Dentistry & Performance Centre

Protecting athletes and optimising oral health for peak performance.

BOOK A CONSULTATION →

The relationship between oral health and athletic performance is more significant than most athletes and coaches realise. Dental pain, gum disease, poorly fitting mouthguards, and bite imbalances can all affect performance, recovery, and concentration. At the same time, the demands of sport — dehydration, acidic sports drinks, physical contact, and stress — create a distinct and elevated oral health risk profile that standard dental care does not account for.

“An athlete's mouth is under more pressure than most. Protecting it — and optimising it — is a performance variable that deserves the same attention as nutrition and training.”

The Sports Programme

What Does It Involve?

Sports dentistry at Vats & Param addresses both the protective and the performance dimensions of oral health in sport. We design and fabricate custom mouthguards that offer genuine protection and wearability, unlike over-the-counter alternatives. We also assess the bite and jaw mechanics of athletes experiencing unexplained performance issues, facial pain, or chronic jaw tension.

We work with sporting academies, school teams, and individual athletes across disciplines. Our dental trauma management pathway ensures rapid, evidence-based care when injuries occur — because the window for optimal outcomes after avulsion or luxation injuries is measured in minutes.

Our Approach

Three Principles Behind Every Appointment

01

Protection is Performance

A dental injury or chronic dental infection can end a season. Preventive care is as important to athletic performance as any other dimension of preparation.

02

Custom Over Generic

Over-the-counter mouthguards offer inadequate protection and impede breathing. Every mouthguard we provide is individually fabricated for a precise, protective fit.

03

Rapid Response

Dental trauma requires immediate management. We maintain a clear emergency pathway so that athletes receive optimal care within the time windows that matter.
