Functional Dentistry

Bite, TMJ & Functional Dentistry

Where jaw mechanics, bite balance, and whole-body health converge.

Your bite is more than how your teeth meet. It is the foundation of your jaw, neck, posture, and sleep. When the bite is off balance, the consequences can range from tooth wear and jaw clicking to chronic headaches, neck pain, and disrupted sleep. Our Bite, TMJ & Functional Dentistry Centre is built to investigate and resolve these patterns at their root.

“Most people with chronic jaw pain, headaches, or tooth grinding have never been told their bite might be the source. That is where our work begins.”

The Functional Assessment

What Does It Involve?

Functional dentistry requires a different kind of consultation. We do not simply look at your teeth; instead, we assess how your jaw moves, how your muscles function at rest and under load, how your bite forces are distributed, and how these patterns are connected to your symptoms and systemic health.

Our team uses detailed occlusal analysis, joint imaging, and muscle assessment to build a complete picture before recommending any treatment. Splints, bite adjustments, and rehabilitation are only prescribed when clinically indicated and fully explained.

12 Functional Services

01 / 12

Bite Analysis

Detailed mapping of occlusal contacts, bite force distribution, and premature contacts to identify functional imbalances.

02 / 12

Occlusal Assessment

Systematic evaluation of how your upper and lower teeth relate in all jaw positions including rest, centric relation, and function.

03 / 12

TMJ Assessment

Clinical evaluation of jaw joint sounds, range of motion, joint loading, and tenderness with imaging where indicated.

04 / 12

Jaw Pain Management

Multi-modal management of temporomandibular joint pain including splints, physiotherapy referral, and targeted occlusal adjustments.

05 / 12

Bruxism Management

Identification of bruxism type and severity with night guards, bite adjustments, stress management, and monitoring protocols.

06 / 12

Clenching Assessment

Assessment of awake and sleep clenching patterns with management strategies to reduce load on teeth and joints.

07 / 12

Night Guards

Custom-fabricated occlusal splints to protect teeth and joints from the damaging effects of nocturnal parafunction.

08 / 12

Splint Therapy

Diagnostic and therapeutic splint construction to decompress the TMJ, reduce muscle activity, and stabilise bite position.

09 / 12

Bite Correction

Targeted occlusal equilibration to eliminate premature contacts and distribute bite forces more evenly across the dental arch.

10 / 12

Occlusal Rehabilitation

Comprehensive bite reconstruction using crowns, veneers, or composite restorations to establish a stable, balanced occlusion.

11 / 12

Full Mouth Rehabilitation

Complete restorative reconstruction of the bite for cases of severe wear, collapse of vertical dimension, or multi-unit tooth loss.

12 / 12

Headache & Facial Pain Assessment

Systematic review of dental and jaw-related contributions to facial pain, migraines, and tension-type headaches.

Our Approach

Three Principles Behind Every Appointment

01

Function Before Aesthetics

We restore how your bite works before we address how it looks. A beautiful smile built on an unstable bite will not last.

02

Whole-Body Thinking

Jaw function connects to posture, breathing, and sleep. Our assessment integrates all these systems rather than treating the jaw in isolation.

03

No Guesswork

Every recommendation in this centre is based on measured evidence, not intuition. We test before we treat and monitor before we conclude.
