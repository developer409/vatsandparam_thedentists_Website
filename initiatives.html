Our Initiatives

Beyond the clinic – our ongoing programs bringing dental care and education to the wider community.

❯ Vatsalya Dental

Vatsalya Dental is a dental clinic chain founded in 2003 by Dr Srivats Bharadwaj with a mission to provide knowledge and education to people about dentistry. With a team of experienced and skilled dental professionals, Vatsalya Dental is committed to delivering high-quality dental care services to patients in a friendly and comfortable environment.

Over the years, Vatsalya Dental has expanded its reach and established itself as a prominent player in the dental industry. The chain of clinics has been recognized as one of the top 5 dental clinics in the world by the Global Clinic Rating (GCR). This recognition is a testament to the quality of dental care services offered by Vatsalya Dental.

❯ Vatsalya Dento Jungle

We understand that many children experience anxiety and fear when it comes to visiting the dentist. That's why we had created Vatsalya Dento Jungle, a theme-based dental clinic specially designed to make dental visits fun and fearless for kids.

Our jungle-themed dental clinic is filled with colorful decorations, friendly animal characters, and interactive games and activities to create a welcoming and engaging environment for children.

We believe that by creating a positive and fun atmosphere, we can help children feel more comfortable and at ease during their dental appointments.

Vatsalya Dento Jungle is staffed by experienced dental professionals who specialize in treating children. Our team understands the unique needs and concerns of young patients and is trained to provide gentle and compassionate care.

❯ Vatsalya Oral Health Foundation

Vatsalya Oral Health Foundation is a non-profit organization established in 2015 with the aim of providing dental care services to underprivileged people of all ages. The foundation offers a range of dental treatments to address the oral healthcare needs of infants, children, adults, and elderly people who cannot afford dental care.

In addition to offering dental treatments, Vatsalya Oral Health Foundation organizes free dental camps in various parts of Karnataka to reach out to people who live in remote areas and have limited access to dental care. These camps provide a platform for people to receive dental check-ups, treatments, and oral health education from experienced dental professionals.

One of the key objectives of the foundation is to raise awareness about the importance of oral hygiene and dental care in preventing dental problems. The foundation provides education on oral hygiene to patients, schools, and communities through various programs and initiatives.

Vatsalya Oral Health Foundation has also organized free dental camps and treatments for tribal people living near Kabini and other remote parts of Karnataka.

The foundation's mission is to make dental care accessible to everyone, regardless of their socioeconomic status. By offering free dental treatments and education, Vatsalya Oral Health Foundation is working towards creating a healthier society with better oral health.

❯ League of Dentists

League of Dentists is an organization that started in 2020 with the aim of mentoring practicing dental professionals and dental students about the art of dentistry. The venture is continuously evolving, and its goal is to provide valuable mentorship to those who are passionate about the field of dentistry.

🎓

Mentoring Since 2020

The League of Dentists has had a significant impact on the careers of many aspiring dentists. By providing mentorship, it has helped to guide dental students and practicing professionals towards success. The mentorship covers a range of topics, including clinical skills, communication skills, and business skills.

One of the key objectives of the League of Dentists is to create a community of dental professionals who can support and learn from one another — fostering a culture of continuous learning and improvement among dental professionals.

Mentorship is provided through various channels, including online workshops, seminars, and one-on-one coaching sessions, led by experienced dental professionals like Dr. Srivats Bharadwaj and Dr. Paramjot Kaur.

❯ Dental Chair for Disabled

At our clinics, we understand that dental care is important for everyone, regardless of their physical abilities. That is why we are proud to offer a wheelchair recliner dental chair for disabled people, designed to allow patients to receive dental treatment while remaining comfortably seated in their wheelchair.

♿

Accessible Care for All

Our wheelchair recliner dental chair is equipped with specialized features that ensure the safety and comfort of disabled patients during dental procedures. The chair reclines to a comfortable position, allowing patients to receive dental treatment while remaining in their wheelchair. The chair also features adjustable headrests and armrests, ensuring that patients are properly supported throughout the procedure.

Our wheelchair recliner dental chair is an important part of our commitment to providing accessible and inclusive dental care to all of our patients. Whether you are disabled or able-bodied, we believe that everyone deserves access to quality dental care, and we are proud to offer specialized solutions to meet the unique needs of our patients.

❯ Dental Clinic for Cancer Patients at HCG

As part of our ongoing efforts to support individuals with unique healthcare needs, we have established a dedicated dental clinic for cancer patients at HCG. The clinic is designed to provide specialized dental care to patients undergoing cancer treatment, as these individuals often face unique oral health challenges.

🏥

Specialised Oncology Dental Care

The dental clinic for cancer patients at HCG is staffed by experienced dental professionals who have received specialized training in providing care to patients undergoing cancer treatment. These professionals are equipped with the knowledge and tools necessary to address the unique oral health challenges faced by cancer patients, such as dry mouth, mouth sores, and gum disease.

❯ Oral Health Educational Initiatives

We believe that education is the key to promoting good oral health and preventing dental problems. As part of our commitment to educating the community about oral health, we had organized several seminars, conferences, and mock treatments to educate children from various schools and colleges.

📚

Education for All

Our team of experienced dental professionals like Dr. Srivats Bharadwaj and Dr. Paramjot Kaur had conducted online seminars during the COVID-19 pandemic to educate people about the importance of oral health. We understand that the pandemic made it difficult for people to access dental care, and we wanted to ensure that people have the knowledge they need to maintain good oral health at home.

We have also conducted seminars at schools located in remote parts of Karnataka to educate children and their families about the importance of oral health. We believe that everyone, regardless of their location or socioeconomic status, deserves access to quality dental care and education.

Our seminars cover a wide range of topics, including proper brushing and flossing techniques, the importance of regular dental checkups, and the effects of diet and lifestyle on oral health. We use interactive and engaging methods to ensure that our audience stays engaged and retains the information they learn.
