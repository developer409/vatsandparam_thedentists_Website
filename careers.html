We're a small, focused practice building a culture of ethical, compassionate, science-driven, whole-body dentistry. We don't chase procedure targets — we care about thoughtful diagnosis, honest communication, and doctors who want to keep growing.

If that sounds like the kind of team you want to be part of, take a look below.

Why work at Vats & Param?

Mentorship & Growth

Real guidance from experienced doctors — honest feedback, structured development, and support in diagnosis, communication and systems thinking.

Purpose-Driven Culture

Whole-body focused, root-cause dentistry — not procedure targets. A team that cares about doing work that actually matters to people.

Continuous Learning

Learning isn't optional here — it's part of how we grow. Openness to feedback and constant upskilling are part of the culture.

Our approach goes beyond procedures

We believe the mouth is connected to the rest of health, and we encourage broader thinking around prevention, function, lifestyle, oral-systemic understanding and long-term wellbeing. In practice, that means we value:

  • Thoughtful diagnosis
  • Strong communication
  • Prevention & education
  • Patient dignity
  • Documentation & discipline
  • Continuous learning

Communication is not separate from dentistry — it is part of dentistry.

Documentation is not paperwork — it is professionalism.

Learning is not optional — it is part of growth.

This environment may not suit everyone.

But if you are looking for more than "just a job" — if you are seeking growth, purpose, mentorship, challenge and meaningful healthcare culture — this conversation may be worth having.

We look forward to meeting you.

Dentist

BDS Graduate (2-3 Years Experience)

Full-time position — Join our team as a clinical dentist ready to grow beyond traditional practice.

Who is this for?

BDS graduates with 2-3 years of clinical experience seeking mentorship, meaningful patient care, and growth in a diagnosis-driven practice.

What we're looking for:

  • Commitment to honest, thoughtful diagnosis and root-cause thinking
  • Strong communication and genuine interest in patient education
  • Openness to feedback and genuine growth mindset
  • Interest in oral-systemic connections and whole-body health
  • Attention to detail and excellent documentation practices
  • Ability to collaborate in a focused, purpose-driven team

What you'll get:

  • Direct mentorship from experienced dentists
  • Structured feedback and professional development
  • Culture that values learning over procedure targets
  • Exposure to diagnostic-driven, whole-body focused dentistry
  • Opportunity to reshape how dentistry is practiced
