Microbiome & Precision Health

Oral Microbiome Diagnostics

Advanced testing of your oral bacterial ecosystem to understand disease risk, guide personalised care, and support whole-body health.

Book a Consultation

The oral microbiome is a complex ecosystem of bacteria, fungi, and viruses that plays a central role in both oral and systemic health. Our diagnostic testing goes beyond standard check-ups to understand your unique microbial profile and identify risk factors for disease.

Microbial Profiling

Microbial Profiling

Salivary and subgingival sampling to identify the specific bacteria present, their relative abundance, and pathogenic potential.

Caries Risk Assessment

Caries Risk Assessment

Identify high-risk bacteria such as Streptococcus mutans and Lactobacillus species that drive tooth decay, enabling targeted prevention.

Periodontal Pathogen Testing

Periodontal Pathogen Testing

Detect keystone periodontal pathogens linked to gum disease, systemic inflammation, cardiovascular risk, and diabetes complications.

Personalised Treatment Planning

Personalised Treatment Planning

Use microbiome data to guide antibiotic selection, probiotic recommendations, and preventive strategies specific to your bacterial profile.
