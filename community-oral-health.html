Public Health & Outreach

Community Oral Health

Extending quality dental education, screening, and preventive care beyond the clinic walls to underserved communities.

Book a Consultation

Oral disease disproportionately affects communities with limited access to dental care. Through outreach, education, and partnerships, we work to extend the benefits of good oral health beyond our clinic to the wider community, focusing on prevention, early detection, and health literacy.

Community Screening Camps
