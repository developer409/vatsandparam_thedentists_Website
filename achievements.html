Our Achievements

Rotary Excellence Award

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj - BDS MDS FADI has been honoured with the Vocational Excellence Award by Rotary Bangalore South for his unwavering dedication and selfless work in promoting oral hygiene and preventive dentistry among the pediatric community. This award is a recognition of his outstanding efforts and commitment to improving one’s oral health.

SOWWG Global Honor

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj has been acknowledged and appreciated for his significant contribution to the success of the 2005 Special Olympics World Winter Games held in Nagano, Japan.

Indian Academy of Orofacial Genetics

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj has been conferred the honor of becoming a life member of the Indian Academy of Orofacial Genetics, with the noble objective of promoting the understanding and dissemination of knowledge related to human genetics specifically in relation to orofacial features.

Specialized Recognition

The Academy of Dentistry International has acknowledged and honored Prof Dr. Srivats Bharadwaj for his noteworthy contributions towards the global progress and development of dentistry.

Healthcare Excellence

The Indian Red Cross Society for Karnataka State Branch has bestowed the honor of a life membership upon Dr Srivats Bharadwaj.

Community Impact

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj has been recognized and appreciated for his participation in the Atraumatic Restorative Treatment (ART) program organized by the United Mission to Nepal Oral Health Programme.

Preventive Care Excellence

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj has been certified as an Honorary Calgarian, thereby extending his ability to offer his special brand of Western hospitality to everyone.

Holistic Healthcare Honor

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj’s valuable contribution to the scientific program of the FDI Annual World Dental Congress held in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia has been acknowledged and appreciated.

Professional Leadership

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj’s selfless contributions to society have been recognized and appreciated by the Lions Club International, which has extended their thanks to him.

CEO India Magazine

Dr Srivats Bharadwaj was featured on the cover for advancing whole-body, connected dentistry and pioneering integrated health solutions.
