Population Care Home Dental Care Services Professional dental care delivered to you, wherever you call home. 
For some people, attending a dental clinic is not simply inconvenient but genuinely impossible. Physical disability, frailty, severe medical illness, or the challenges of caring for someone who cannot travel can all make clinic-based dental care inaccessible. Our Home Dental Care Services bring professional, evidence-based dental assessment and treatment directly to you, whether you are at home, in a care facility, or a residential setting. "No one should go without dental care because they cannot reach us. We designed our home service to remove every barrier that stands between good oral health and the people who need it most." 
The Home Care Programme What Does It Involve? Home dental care visits are conducted using portable, fully equipped dental equipment that allows us to provide a wide range of services outside the clinic setting. We bring everything needed for assessment, preventive care, and many treatment procedures directly to you. Our clinical standards do not change when we leave the clinic; only our location changes. Appointments are planned with full consideration of your home environment, mobility requirements, and any carer involvement needed. We work carefully and respectfully in your personal space, communicating clearly at every stage. Families and carers are welcome to be present and involved in the consultation. 
7 Home Dental Services 

01 / 07 Home Consultations Comprehensive dental assessments conducted at your home using portable diagnostic equipment, producing a full written report and treatment plan. 
02 / 07 Preventive Dental Care Scaling, polishing, fluoride application, and oral hygiene instruction delivered in your home environment by our clinical team. 
03 / 07 Oral Hygiene Support Practical, individualised guidance for patients and their carers on maintaining effective oral hygiene routines at home. 
04 / 07 Elderly Care Adapted dental care for frail elderly individuals, including those with cognitive impairment, complex medical histories, or reduced mobility. 
05 / 07 Bedridden Patient Care Oral assessment and basic dental management for individuals who are bed-bound, including oral care protocols for prolonged bed rest. 
06 / 07 Medically Compromised Patient Care Dental care coordination for patients with complex medical conditions for whom travel to a clinic would present a significant clinical risk. 
07 / 07 Caregiver Training Hands-on training for family carers, support workers, and nursing staff on oral hygiene techniques for dependent adults and children.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 01 Care Without Barriers Dental care is a right, not a privilege reserved for those who can access a clinic. Our home service ensures that geography, mobility, and health are never barriers. 02 Clinical Standards Maintained Portable equipment and different environments do not mean lower standards. We deliver the same quality of care and documentation outside the clinic as within it. 03 Dignity in Your Space We work respectfully within your home and adapt to your environment, routines, and preferences, not the other way around.
