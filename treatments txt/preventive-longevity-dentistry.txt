Preventive & Longevity Dentistry 
Stopping problems before they start. Sustaining your smile for life. 

The most valuable dental care you will ever receive is the care that prevents a problem from developing in the first place. Preventive & Longevity Dentistry at Vats & Param goes far beyond a cleaning appointment. We use evidence-based risk assessment, early intervention, and long-term monitoring to keep your teeth, gums, and whole body healthier for longer. 
"Treating disease is reactive. Preventing it is wisdom. Our goal is a future where your dental visits are uneventful, which requires work today." 

The Programme 
What Does It Involve? 

Our preventive programmes are built around individual risk profiles, not generic protocols. We look at your susceptibility to decay, gum disease, acid erosion, and tooth wear, then build a plan that addresses your specific vulnerabilities rather than assuming a one-size approach. 
Preventive appointments are not passive check-ups. They are proactive, detailed reviews that track change over time and allow us to intervene early, when treatment is simpler, less invasive, and more effective. 

12 Preventive Services 

01 / 12 Preventive Dental Examinations Regular structured assessments focused on catching early signs of disease, changes in the oral environment, and evolving risk factors. 
02 / 12 Professional Cleaning Expert removal of plaque and calculus from all tooth surfaces, including areas that brushing and flossing cannot reach. 
03 / 12 Scaling & Polishing Deep cleaning of root surfaces to remove build-up and reduce the bacterial load that drives gum inflammation. 
04 / 12 Fluoride Therapy Targeted application of fluoride to strengthen enamel, reduce sensitivity, and protect high-risk surfaces from decay. 
05 / 12 Sealants Protective resin coatings applied to vulnerable grooves in back teeth to prevent caries in areas prone to decay. 
06 / 12 Caries Risk Assessment A structured evaluation of your biological, dietary, and behavioural risk factors for tooth decay with a personalised management plan. 
07 / 12 Tooth Wear Assessment Monitoring and analysis of erosion, abrasion, and attrition to identify causes and prevent accelerated tooth loss. 
08 / 12 Dry Mouth Management Assessment and management of xerostomia and salivary dysfunction, which significantly increase risk of decay and infection. 
09 / 12 Oral Hygiene Coaching Personalised one-to-one instruction on brushing technique, interdental cleaning, and evidence-based home care products. 
10 / 12 Dietary Counselling Assessment of dietary habits and their impact on acid erosion, sugar exposure, and oral microbiome balance. 
11 / 12 Recall & Maintenance Programs Structured review intervals matched to your risk level to ensure monitoring is consistent and timely. 
12 / 12 Healthy Ageing Oral Care Proactive planning for the oral health changes that occur with age, including bone loss, dry mouth, and medication effects.

Our Approach 
Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Prevention First We do not wait for problems to become obvious. Early detection and personalised risk reduction is the foundation of everything we recommend. 
02 Evidence-Based Protocols Every recommendation is grounded in current research, not habit. We review the evidence and apply it to your individual risk profile, not a default checklist. 
03 Long-Term Partnership Prevention works best over time. We build a relationship with your oral health history, tracking trends and making adjustments as your life and health evolve.
