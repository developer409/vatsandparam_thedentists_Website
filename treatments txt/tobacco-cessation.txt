Assessments & Diagnostics Tobacco Cessation & Oral Cancer Prevention Supporting tobacco users to stop, and protecting everyone from oral cancer. 
Tobacco in all its forms, whether smoked, smokeless, or inhaled, is the single largest modifiable risk factor for oral cancer and periodontal disease, and a significant contributor to implant failure, treatment complications, and poor wound healing. Our Tobacco Cessation & Oral Cancer Prevention Centre combines cessation counselling, structured stop-smoking programmes, and vigilant oral cancer surveillance into a single, integrated service. "Stopping tobacco use is the single most impactful thing most of our patients can do for their oral and systemic health. Our role is to provide the expertise and support to make that achievable." 
The Cessation & Prevention Programme What Does It Involve? Tobacco cessation counselling at Vats & Param is conducted by trained clinicians using evidence-based brief intervention and motivational interviewing approaches. We do not lecture or judge; instead, we provide information, explore readiness to change, and offer structured support including pharmacotherapy referral, follow-up appointments, and relapse management. Oral cancer screening is part of every comprehensive oral assessment, and in tobacco users it is conducted with heightened vigilance. Any suspicious lesion is documented, monitored, and biopsied where indicated. Our referral pathway to oral medicine and secondary care is clear and fast. 
7 Services 

01 / 07 Tobacco Cessation Counselling Individual brief intervention sessions using motivational interviewing to assess readiness to quit and develop a personalised cessation plan. 
02 / 07 Smoking Cessation Programs Structured multi-session cessation programmes including behavioural support, pharmacotherapy referral, and follow-up to support sustained abstinence. 
03 / 07 Smokeless Tobacco Intervention Specific counselling and lesion monitoring for users of pan, betel nut, gutka, snuff, and other smokeless tobacco forms common in the Indian context. 
04 / 07 Oral Cancer Screening Systematic examination of all oral soft tissues for leukoplakia, erythroplakia, submucous fibrosis, and other potentially malignant conditions in tobacco users. 
05 / 07 Oral Potentially Malignant Disorder Monitoring Regular clinical and photographic monitoring of diagnosed precancerous lesions with biopsy and referral protocols for lesions showing changes. 
06 / 07 Community Awareness Programs Public education and community outreach on the oral and systemic health risks of tobacco use, tailored for schools, workplaces, and community settings. 
07 / 07 Workplace Screening Programs Onsite tobacco screening and oral cancer prevention programmes for corporate and institutional partners, including individual consultations and group education.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 01 Evidence-Based Support Our cessation approach is grounded in established clinical guidelines. Pharmacotherapy, behaviour change, and motivational support are all tools we draw on. 02 Zero Judgement Tobacco dependence is a medical condition, not a moral failing. Our cessation consultations are supportive, non-judgmental, and focused entirely on your goals. 03 Surveillance as Standard For all tobacco users, oral cancer screening is integrated into every visit as a non-negotiable clinical standard.
