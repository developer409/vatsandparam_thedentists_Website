Smile Design & Aesthetic Dentistry 
Crafting smiles that express who you are, with nothing that feels artificial. 

Aesthetic dentistry at its best is invisible. You should not be able to tell that anything was done, only that the person in front of you looks exceptional, confident, and completely natural. Smile Design at Vats & Param begins not with a drill but with a conversation about what you want, what you do not want, and what is realistically achievable. We use digital smile planning, mock-ups, and reversible trials before committing to anything irreversible. 
"The best aesthetic result is one that suits the person entirely, not the trend of the moment. Timeless confidence is always our goal." 

The Smile Design Programme What Does It Involve? 
Smile Design is a structured process. We analyse your facial proportions, the natural characteristics of your existing teeth, the colour and texture of your gum tissue, and the way your smile moves when you speak and laugh. Digital smile planning allows us to show you a preview of the expected result before treatment begins, giving you input and confidence in the process. 
Our aesthetic work ranges from minimally invasive composite bonding and whitening to porcelain veneers and full aesthetic rehabilitation. We always favour the most conservative approach that achieves your goals, because every irreversible step deserves careful consideration. 

6 Aesthetic Services 

01 / 06 Smile Design A structured facial and dental analysis process that maps your ideal smile proportions and formulates a personalised treatment plan. 
02 / 06 Veneers Ultra-thin porcelain or composite facings bonded to the front surface of teeth to transform colour, shape, and symmetry with minimal tooth reduction. 
03 / 06 Tooth Whitening Professional in-clinic and home-based whitening protocols using clinically validated bleaching agents to lighten teeth safely and effectively. 
04 / 06 Cosmetic Bonding Direct application of tooth-coloured composite resin to repair chips, close gaps, and reshape teeth in a single appointment with minimal intervention. 
05 / 06 Digital Smile Planning Computer-generated smile preview technology that allows you to visualise and approve your expected results before any irreversible treatment is begun. 
06 / 06 Aesthetic Rehabilitation Comprehensive smile reconstruction combining whitening, bonding, veneers, and restorations to achieve a complete transformation in cases of widespread aesthetic concerns.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Conservation Over Reduction We preserve natural tooth structure wherever possible. The most conservative treatment that achieves your goal is always the right one. 
02 Your Smile, Not a Template We design around your face, your personality, and your goals — not a catalogue of standard shapes and shades. 
03 Preview Before You Commit Digital planning and composite mock-ups mean you see the expected result before any permanent work begins. No surprises.
