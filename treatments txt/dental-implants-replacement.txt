Dental Implants & Tooth Replacement 
Replacing missing teeth with a solution that looks, feels, and functions like your own. 

When a tooth is lost, the consequences extend well beyond the gap. Bone begins to resorb, adjacent teeth shift, bite mechanics change, and over time, face shape can alter. Dental implants are the closest modern dentistry can come to replacing a natural tooth root. They preserve bone, restore function, and eliminate the compromises of bridges and dentures. Our Dental Implants & Tooth Replacement Centre provides a complete pathway from assessment to final restoration. 
"An implant is not just a tooth. It is a signal to the jawbone to maintain its volume, its form, and its architecture. That signal matters for decades." 

The Implant Programme What Does It Involve? 
Implant planning at Vats & Param begins with a thorough assessment of your bone volume, gum health, bite, and systemic health. We use cone beam CT scanning and digital planning software to map the ideal implant position before treatment begins. Where bone volume is insufficient, we offer grafting procedures to create the foundation needed for a successful, long-lasting result. 
The implant journey is explained clearly at every stage: surgery, healing, restoration, and long-term maintenance. We work with carefully selected restorative materials and prosthetic components to ensure that the final crown, bridge, or full-arch solution meets your functional and aesthetic expectations. 

8 Implant Services 

01 / 08 Implant Assessment Comprehensive evaluation of bone volume, gum health, bite mechanics, and systemic health to assess implant candidacy and plan treatment. 
02 / 08 Implant Placement Surgical placement of titanium implant fixtures into the jawbone under local anaesthesia, using precision-guided surgical protocols. 
03 / 08 Bone Grafting Augmentation of deficient bone sites using autogenous bone, synthetic graft materials, or xenografts to create an adequate implant foundation. 
04 / 08 Sinus Lift Procedures Elevation of the sinus membrane and placement of bone graft in the upper jaw to enable implant placement where bone height is insufficient. 
05 / 08 Implant Restorations Fabrication and placement of crowns, bridges, or implant-supported prostheses onto integrated implants using precision ceramic components. 
06 / 08 Dentures Conventional and implant-retained full and partial dentures designed for comfort, retention, and aesthetics. 
07 / 08 Bridges Fixed implant-supported bridges providing a stable, non-removable solution for multiple missing teeth without the need for individual implants at each position. 
08 / 08 Full Arch Rehabilitation Complete jaw reconstruction using implant-supported fixed bridges, including All-on-4 and All-on-6 approaches for patients with multiple or complete tooth loss.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment
