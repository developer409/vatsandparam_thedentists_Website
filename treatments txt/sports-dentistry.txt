Functional Dentistry 
Sports Dentistry & Performance Centre 
Protecting athletes and optimising oral health for peak performance. 

The relationship between oral health and athletic performance is more significant than most athletes and coaches realise. Dental pain, gum disease, poorly fitting mouthguards, and bite imbalances can all affect performance, recovery, and concentration. At the same time, the demands of sport (such as dehydration, acidic sports drinks, physical contact, and stress) create a distinct and elevated oral health risk profile that standard dental care does not account for. 
"An athlete&apos;s mouth is under more pressure than most. Protecting and optimising it is a performance variable that deserves the same attention as nutrition and training." 

The Sports Programme What Does It Involve? 
Sports dentistry at Vats & Param addresses both the protective and the performance dimensions of oral health in sport. We design and fabricate custom mouthguards that offer genuine protection and wearability, unlike over-the-counter alternatives. We also assess the bite and jaw mechanics of athletes experiencing unexplained performance issues, facial pain, or chronic jaw tension. 
We work with sporting academies, school teams, and individual athletes across disciplines. Our dental trauma management pathway ensures rapid, evidence-based care when injuries occur, as the window for optimal outcomes after avulsion or luxation injuries is measured in minutes. 

7 Sports Dentistry Services 

01 / 07 Sports Dental Assessments Comprehensive oral assessment tailored to the specific risk profile of athletes, including acid erosion, trauma history, and bite mechanics. 
02 / 07 Custom Mouthguards Precision-fitted protective mouthguards fabricated from athlete-specific models, offering far superior protection and comfort to over-the-counter alternatives. 
03 / 07 Athletic Performance Oral Health Reviews Assessment of how oral health factors including gum disease, bite imbalance, and dental infection may be contributing to performance or recovery issues. 
04 / 07 Dental Trauma Prevention Education and equipment protocols to reduce the risk of dental injuries across different sport types and competition levels. 
05 / 07 Emergency Sports Dental Care Rapid-response management of traumatic dental injuries including avulsed teeth, fractured crowns, and soft tissue lacerations. 
06 / 07 Hydration & Oral Health Counselling Evidence-based guidance on the impact of sports drinks, gels, and hydration practices on tooth erosion and decay risk. 
07 / 07 Recovery-Focused Oral Health Programs Long-term oral health management for athletes that accounts for training cycles, nutritional supplements, and the physiological demands of elite sport.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Protection is Performance A dental injury or chronic dental infection can end a season. Preventive care is as important to athletic performance as any other dimension of preparation. 
02 Custom Over Generic Over-the-counter mouthguards offer inadequate protection and impede breathing. Every mouthguard we provide is individually fabricated for a precise, protective fit. 
03 Rapid Response Dental trauma requires immediate management. We maintain a clear emergency pathway so that athletes receive optimal care within the time windows that matter.
