Accessibility & Inclusion 
Dental Care for Wheelchair-Bound Patients 
We believe everyone deserves access to world-class dental care. Our clinics are equipped with specialized wheelchair recliner dental chairs to ensure a seamless, comfortable experience. 

Inclusive Technology 
The Compact Wheelchair Recliner Dental Chair 

For wheelchair-bound patients, visiting the dentist can often be a challenge. Many dental offices are not designed to accommodate patients in wheelchairs, and traditional dental chairs can be uncomfortable or impossible for them to use. We have integrated advanced wheelchair-based treatment solutions at all our clinics. 

What is it? 
A compact wheelchair recliner dental chair is designed to provide comfort and accessibility to wheelchair-bound patients during dental procedures. Smaller in size than traditional units, it has the ability to recline, allowing patients to remain seated in their own wheelchair while receiving treatment. The compact design also makes it easier for our specialists to maneuver and provide high-precision care without requiring stressful transfers. 

Why it Matters 
Benefits of Accessible Care 

Improved Comfort 

Improved Comfort 
The reclining feature provides additional support during treatment. Patients remain in their own familiar wheelchair, avoiding the discomfort and risk of being transferred. 

Increased Accessibility 

Increased Accessibility 
Our clinics are designed to eliminate barriers. This chair ensures that patients who require specialized care can receive it without logistical stress. 

Enhanced Safety 

Enhanced Safety 
Equipped with adjustable headrests, armrests, and specialized seat belts to ensure stability and safety throughout the clinical procedure. 

Reduced Anxiety 

Reduced Anxiety 
Providing a familiar and comfortable environment helps reduce clinical anxiety, making the patient feel more at ease during their visit. 

Questions & Answers 
Frequently Asked Questions 

Does your clinic have a wheelchair recliner dental chair?

Yes, we have specialized wheelchair recliner arrangements at all our clinics to ensure every patient receives the care they need comfortably. 

What is a wheelchair recliner dental chair?

It is a specialized clinical unit that allows a patient's wheelchair to be securely positioned and reclined for dental work, eliminating the need for a chair transfer. 

Is there an additional cost to use this chair?

No, providing accessible care is part of our commitment to all patients. There is no additional fee for utilizing our wheelchair-accessible facilities. 

Will it affect the quality of my dental care?

On the contrary, it often improves the quality of care by ensuring the patient is stable, comfortable, and properly positioned for the dentist to work with maximum precision.
