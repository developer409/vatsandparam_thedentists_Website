Children&apos;s Oral Health & Development 
Building healthy smiles from the very first tooth. 

A child&apos;s oral health is not just about baby teeth. It is about the foundation for a lifetime of wellbeing. From the first tooth to the transition to adult dentition, the decisions made during childhood and adolescence shape jaw development, airway health, bite function, and confidence. Our Children&apos;s Oral Health & Development Centre is designed to give every child the right start. 
"The most important dental appointment a child has is the one that never leads to a problem in the first place. Prevention in childhood pays dividends for life." 

The Children&apos;s Programme What Does It Involve? 
Paediatric dentistry at Vats & Param is built on early assessment, gentle intervention, and family education. We assess children in a calm, child-friendly environment, taking the time needed to build trust and make each visit a positive one. Our focus is on growth-pattern monitoring, habit counselling, and interceptive care that prevents more complex treatment later. 
We work alongside parents to establish excellent home care, healthy dietary habits, and a positive relationship with dental visits from the earliest age. Children who feel safe at the dentist are more likely to maintain their oral health for life. 

9 Paediatric Services 

01 / 09 Infant Oral Health Guidance on teething, dummy habits, bottle feeding, and early oral hygiene for parents of infants before the first tooth erupts. 
02 / 09 Preventive Paediatric Dentistry Fissure sealants, fluoride applications, and structured preventive reviews tailored to the specific risk profile of each child. 
03 / 09 Early Childhood Caries Care Early identification and minimally invasive management of caries in primary dentition, including Hall technique and silver diamine fluoride. 
04 / 09 Growth & Development Assessments Regular monitoring of jaw growth, eruption sequences, and bite patterns to identify deviations that warrant early intervention. 
05 / 09 Habit Counselling Assessment and management of thumb sucking, dummy use, mouth breathing, and tongue thrusting habits that affect jaw and airway development. 
06 / 09 Interceptive Orthodontics Early orthodontic intervention to guide jaw development, correct crossbites, and create space for erupting permanent teeth. 
07 / 09 Airway-Focused Screening Assessment of nasal breathing, tonsil size, tongue posture, and palate form to identify airway compromise during the growth years. 
08 / 09 Sports Mouthguards Custom-fitted mouthguards for children involved in contact and collision sports to prevent dental trauma. 
09 / 09 Preventive Programs Structured oral health education programs tailored to children and their families to establish lifelong healthy habits.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Child-Centred Care Every appointment is adapted to the individual child. We build trust before we begin treatment and never push a child beyond their comfort level. 
02 Prevention Over Intervention Our highest goal is a dental history with nothing to treat. We invest in prevention so that intervention becomes the exception, not the routine. 
03 Family Partnership Parents are essential partners in a child&apos;s oral health. We educate and involve families in every plan we make.
