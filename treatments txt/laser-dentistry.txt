Laser Dentistry 
A minimally invasive approach to dental care, using highly focused light for precision treatment with less pain, reduced bleeding, and faster healing. 

Overview 
What is Laser Dentistry? 

Laser dentistry is a minimally invasive approach to treating a variety of dental conditions using a highly focused beam of light. It can be used to treat gum disease, remove decayed or damaged tissue, and even whiten teeth. 
Compared to traditional dental treatments, laser dentistry provides more precise results with less pain, bleeding, and swelling . It also allows for faster healing and reduces the need for anesthesia. Overall, laser dentistry is a safe and effective option for many dental procedures. 

Why Choose Laser Dentistry? 
Laser dentistry represents the future of dental care, offering significant advantages over traditional methods for both patients and practitioners. 

1 Precision Targeting 
Highly precise technology that can target specific areas of the mouth , reducing damage to surrounding healthy tissue and preserving more of your natural tooth structure. The focused beam works with microscopic accuracy. 

2 Minimally Invasive 
Unlike traditional drills that can cause damage to healthy tissue and lead to bleeding, pain, and swelling, laser dentistry uses a focused beam of light to gently and precisely treat dental conditions, resulting in significantly less discomfort. 

✓ Faster Healing 
Laser treatment speeds up the healing process and significantly reduces the risk of post-procedure infection. The laser cauterizes as it works, minimizing bleeding during and after treatment, getting you back to normal faster.

Frequently Asked Questions 

Is laser dentistry safe? + 

Yes, laser dentistry is a well-established, FDA-approved technology that has been used in clinical practice for decades. When performed by trained professionals, it is extremely safe and offers numerous advantages over traditional methods. 

Does laser dentistry hurt? + 

Most patients find laser procedures significantly more comfortable than traditional treatments. Many laser procedures require little to no anesthesia, and patients experience less post-procedure discomfort and swelling. 

What dental conditions can be treated with lasers? + 

Laser dentistry can be used for a wide range of treatments including gum disease treatment, cavity removal, teeth whitening, gum reshaping, biopsy, removal of lesions or ulcers, and treating tooth sensitivity. Our team will advise whether laser treatment is suitable for your specific condition. 

Is laser dentistry suitable for children? + 

Yes, laser dentistry can be particularly beneficial for pediatric patients as it is less intimidating than traditional drills, reduces the need for anesthesia, and minimizes pain and anxiety during procedures. 

How long does a laser dental procedure take? + 

Laser procedures are often faster than their traditional counterparts. The exact duration depends on the type and complexity of the treatment, but many procedures can be completed in a single, shorter appointment. 

Does insurance cover laser dentistry? + 

Coverage varies by insurance provider and plan. Since laser dentistry is used for many of the same procedures covered by traditional methods, it may be partially or fully covered. We recommend checking with your insurance provider, and our team is happy to assist with any questions.
