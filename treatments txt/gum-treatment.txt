Gum Health & Periodontal Care 
Protecting the foundation your teeth depend on. 

Gum disease is the leading cause of tooth loss in adults, and its impact extends far beyond the mouth. Strong, proven links connect periodontitis and systemic conditions, including heart disease, diabetes, respiratory disease, and adverse pregnancy outcomes, meaning that treating your gums is treating your whole body. Our Gum Health & Periodontal Care Centre provides diagnosis, treatment, and long-term maintenance at every stage. 
"Bleeding gums are not normal. They are a sign that something in the body is out of balance, and that is exactly where we begin." 

The Periodontal Programme 
What Does It Involve? 

Periodontal treatment at Vats & Param begins with a complete and detailed assessment of your gum tissues, bone levels, and bacterial environment. We use pocket charting, digital radiography, and in some cases microbiome analysis to understand the precise nature and extent of disease before developing a treatment plan. 
Treatment ranges from non-surgical deep cleaning to laser therapy and regenerative procedures, depending on the stage and pattern of disease. Every case is followed by a structured maintenance programme to prevent recurrence and monitor your response over time. 

8 Periodontal Services 

01 / 08 Gum Disease Assessment Comprehensive periodontal charting with pocket depth measurements, bone level analysis, and bleeding scores to classify disease stage and grade. 
02 / 08 Scaling & Root Planing Deep cleaning of root surfaces below the gumline to remove bacterial biofilm, calculus, and toxins, allowing tissue healing to begin. 
03 / 08 Periodontal Therapy Non-surgical treatment protocols including root debridement, antimicrobial therapy, and host modulation strategies tailored to disease severity. 
04 / 08 Maintenance Programs Structured recall intervals designed around your disease risk and treatment response to prevent recurrence and monitor stability. 
05 / 08 Laser Gum Therapy Minimally invasive laser-assisted periodontal treatment to decontaminate tissue, reduce pocket depth, and promote regeneration with less discomfort. 
06 / 08 Regenerative Procedures Surgical regenerative techniques using bone grafts and membrane technology to rebuild bone and tissue lost to periodontal disease. 
07 / 08 Peri-Implant Disease Management Specialised assessment and treatment of inflammation and bone loss around dental implants, which follows distinct patterns from natural tooth disease. 
08 / 08 Implant Maintenance Long-term monitoring and professional cleaning of implant surfaces to protect against peri-implantitis and maintain implant longevity.

Our Approach 
Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Root-Cause First We investigate why gum disease has developed, including systemic contributors like diabetes, smoking, stress, and nutrition, not just the local bacterial factors. 
02 Systemic Awareness Periodontal disease is a systemic condition expressed in the mouth. Our management plan always addresses both local and whole-body contributing factors. 
03 Lifelong Maintenance Treatment is only the beginning. A structured, evidence-based maintenance programme is essential to prevent recurrence and protect the investment of treatment.
