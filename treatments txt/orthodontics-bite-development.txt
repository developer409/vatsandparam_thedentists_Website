Orthodontics & Bite Development 
Aligning not just teeth, but jaw function, airway, and whole-body health. 

Straight teeth are not simply a cosmetic goal. They are a structural one. The way your teeth align affects how your jaw functions, how you breathe, how much wear your enamel sustains over time, and even how well you sleep. Orthodontic treatment at Vats & Param is planned and delivered with function, growth, and long-term health at the centre, not just appearance. 
"Moving teeth is the easy part of orthodontics. Understanding where to move them, and why, requires a comprehensive knowledge of jaw mechanics, airway, and facial growth that goes far beyond a digital scan." 

The Orthodontic Programme What Does It Involve? 
Every orthodontic journey at Vats & Param begins with a comprehensive diagnostic workup. We take records including photographs, study models, digital X-rays, and bite analysis before making any treatment recommendation. For children, we evaluate growth potential to time intervention for maximum effectiveness. For adults, we consider bite stability, gum health, and joint function before beginning tooth movement. 
Our treatment options range from traditional braces to clear aligner systems, with a focus on finishing positions that are both aesthetically pleasing and functionally stable. Retention is treated as a permanent part of the programme, not an afterthought. 

8 Orthodontic Services 

01 / 08 Orthodontic Assessments Comprehensive diagnostic evaluation including cephalometric analysis, jaw function assessment, and growth appraisal to plan treatment goals. 
02 / 08 Growth Guidance Timing-sensitive orthodontic interventions during childhood and adolescence that work with jaw growth rather than against it. 
03 / 08 Interceptive Orthodontics Early treatment to correct developing problems before they become structural issues requiring more complex treatment later. 
04 / 08 Braces Fixed orthodontic appliances providing precise tooth control for complex movements in adolescents and adults. 
05 / 08 Clear Aligners Removable transparent aligners offering a discreet, comfortable alternative to fixed braces for appropriate cases. 
06 / 08 Retention Programs Permanent retention planning using fixed and removable retainers to maintain orthodontic results long-term. 
07 / 08 Bite Correction Targeted orthodontic movements to resolve deep bites, open bites, and crossbites that affect function and jaw health. 
08 / 08 Functional Orthodontics Appliance-based therapy that works on jaw development and muscle function, particularly effective during active growth phases.

Our Approach Three Principles Behind Every Appointment 

01 Function Drives Form We plan every case around where the teeth need to be for optimal bite function and airway support, then achieve the aesthetic outcome within that framework. 
02 Timing Matters The right orthodontic intervention at the wrong age produces mediocre results. We time our interventions around your biology, not your schedule. 
03 Retention for Life Treatment results are only as permanent as the retention plan that follows. We design retention into every case from the start.
